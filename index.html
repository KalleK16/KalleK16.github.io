📄 Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. General Information

Your privacy is important to me. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled within the "Travel Learner" mobile application.

2. Data Collection within the App

The "Travel Learner" app itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal user data. All of your study statistics, unlocked destinations, and collected souvenir magnets are stored exclusively local on your device (via SwiftData) and never leave your phone.

3. Third-Party Services (Google AdMob & UMP)

To provide this app for free and enable the feature to shorten flight times, the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) are integrated. These third-party services may process device identifiers (such as the IDFA under iOS), IP addresses, and advertising data to serve ads and manage user consent in compliance with privacy regulations (including GDPR).

Upon launching the app for the the first time, an official Google consent banner will ask for your preferences regarding personalized advertising. You can review or change this choice at any time within your iOS Settings.

4. Your Rights

Since no data is ever transmitted to external servers or hosted by the developer, there is no personal data to access, rectify, or delete. For any questions regarding privacy, please contact the developer via the support email address listed above.
